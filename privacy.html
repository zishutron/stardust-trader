Privacy Policy

Effective: September 13, 2026 Last updated: September 13, 2026
Stardust Trader is a paper-trading simulation. No real money, real brokerage accounts, or real financial transactions are involved at any point. This policy explains what limited information we collect to operate the simulator, and how we handle it.

01Overview

Stardust Trader ("we", "us", "the App") is an educational paper-trading platform. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the App. By using Stardust Trader, you agree to the practices described here.

Because Stardust Trader is a simulation, the scope of data we handle is intentionally minimal. We do not collect payment information, banking details, or any real financial account data — because none of that is ever required to use the App.

02Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — used to create and authenticate your account
  • Display name — chosen by you, shown in the App
  • Account creation date
  • Authentication identifiers issued by Firebase Authentication

Simulated Trading Data

  • Your simulated paper-account balance (starting at $10,000 virtual funds)
  • Simulated holdings, trade history, and pending orders
  • Simulated profit/loss figures and performance statistics
  • Achievements unlocked through simulated activity
  • Watchlist selections, price alerts, and preferences

App Preferences

  • Theme preference (dark or light mode)
  • Notification toggles and confirmation settings
  • Learning-center progress

We do not collect: real payment information, banking details, government IDs, location data, contacts, photos, or any real financial account information.

03How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to operate the paper-trading simulation:

  • Authenticate you and keep you signed in
  • Persist your simulated portfolio, trade history, and preferences across sessions
  • Display your simulated performance and statistics
  • Deliver in-app notifications for simulated events (orders filled, alerts triggered, achievements unlocked)
  • Improve app stability and fix bugs

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond operating the App.

04Third-Party Services

Stardust Trader relies on the following third-party services to function:

Firebase (Google)

  • Firebase Authentication — manages secure email/password sign-in
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your simulated trading data and preferences

Firebase processes data on Google Cloud infrastructure. Google's privacy practices apply to the underlying infrastructure. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Content Delivery

  • Font Awesome (icons) and Lightweight Charts (charting) are loaded via public CDNs
  • These CDNs may log standard technical request data (IP address, user agent) as part of normal web delivery

05Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Each user's data is isolated under their own authenticated user ID, and access is restricted so that one user cannot read another user's data.

We use Firebase Authentication for sign-in. Passwords are never stored in our database — they are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication using industry-standard secure hashing.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we follow reasonable practices, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Do not reuse a real banking password for this App.

06No Financial Transactions

Stardust Trader never processes real money. Specifically:

  • No deposits, withdrawals, or payments are accepted
  • No real brokerage account is connected
  • No real orders are ever placed on any exchange or market
  • All balances, prices, and trades are simulated for educational purposes

Because there are no real financial transactions, we never collect or store payment credentials of any kind.

07Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers. Data is only processed by the third-party services listed in Section 04, strictly to operate the App.

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect the safety and rights of users or the public.

08Your Rights & Controls

Access & Correction

  • You can view and update your display name from the Profile → Edit Profile section
  • You can view all stored simulated trading data within the App

Reset Simulated Data

  • You can reset your paper account (balance, holdings, trades, statistics) from Settings → Reset Paper Account

Delete Your Account

  • You can permanently delete your account from Settings → Delete Account
  • Deletion removes your Firebase Authentication account and all associated Firestore data
  • This action is irreversible

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights (such as data portability or the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority). To exercise any such right, contact us using the details in Section 11.

09Children's Privacy

Stardust Trader is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
